02 / Core loop
Comfort first

Keep the horizon still.

The rider stays seated and the camera follows the headset naturally. The course scrolls past the rider; acceleration is conveyed by world flow and HUD, not camera shake or artificial translation.

Hand controlsTracked joint movement for drive and steer, hand pinch for actions.
